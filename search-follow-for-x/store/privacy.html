Privacy Policy

Search & Follow for X (Chrome extension) · Last updated 22 November 2026

Summary

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything it stores stays in your own browser.

What is stored, and where

Using Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device only, the extension saves:

  • your settings (search text, per-run limit, daily cap, filter options);
  • the state of the current run and a count of follows made today;
  • a short activity log (for example, "followed @handle") shown in the popup.

This data never leaves your browser. Removing the extension deletes it.

What is not collected

The extension has no server, uses no analytics or tracking, and makes no network requests of its own. It does not read your messages, passwords, cookies or browsing history.

Access to x.com

The extension runs only on x.com, stays idle until you press Start, and is used only to open the search, read the account results shown there and click the Follow button when you start a run. Your use of X remains subject to X's own terms and privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
